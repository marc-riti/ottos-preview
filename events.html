Before you go

  • 21+ at every show. Bring ID.Door staff check, every time.
  • Most shows are free.A few nights have a drink minimum or pass the hat. It's on each listing.
  • Tiki drinks: $18 in a pint glass, $28 in a mug.The mug price includes a $10 deposit: keep the mug or bring it back for your $10.
  • It gets loud when bands play.
