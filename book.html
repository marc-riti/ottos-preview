Book the back room

Rockabilly, punk, surf, ska, country, comedy, poetry: Otto's back room has hosted all of it since 2002. Bands, comics, poets, DJs and promoters pitch by email.

How to pitch

Email ottosinfo@gmail.com with:

Hi Otto's, Act: [name] What we do: [genre / comedy / reading / DJ] From: [city] Listen/watch: [links] Dates we'd like: [dates] Good fit for: [a regular night, if any] Thanks!

The room

  • Small stage, tight space.There's limited room for gear. Bands can bring their own, but think house party, not concert hall.
  • Most shows are free to get in.Some nights ask the crowd to tip bands in cash or Venmo.
  • 21+ only.Every event, no exceptions.

House backlineUnconfirmed: House backline (amps, drum kit, PA). See sources

From the bar's old FAQ. Confirm when you book.

  • Guitar: Peavey Classic 50 2x12 combo; Marshall JCM 900 50-watt head with 2x12 cab
  • Bass: Ampeg SVT-3 Pro or Yorkville 400-watt head, 1x15 cab
  • Drums: Pearl kit (rack tom, floor tom, kick) with hi-hat stand, three cymbal stands and throne
  • PA: 8-channel Mackie mixer, three mics and stands

Bring: snare, cymbals, kick pedal, a clutch for the hi-hat, and your instruments.
